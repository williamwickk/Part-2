Our Story

VINTAGE-THREADS is a Johannesburg-based online store specializing in rare designer archive denim, longsleeves, and tees.

We curate pieces from Undercover, Dior, Hedi Slimane, Balenciaga Lost Tapes, Raf Simons, Number (N)ine.

Our Mission +

To make authentic archive fashion accessible and sustainable in South Africa.
